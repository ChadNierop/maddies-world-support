A little help for your little world.

Maddie’s World brings animal charades, coloring, letters, and numbers together for children and their grown-ups.

Contact support

Email chadwick.nierop@gmail.com for help or a personalization inquiry. Tell us your device model, app version, and what happened. Please leave out children’s full names, birthdays, photos, and other sensitive information.

Expansion 1

Garden Friends, Dinosaurs, and Holidays are included together in one optional purchase. Open the Parent Zone for purchases and restoring access. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Drawings and profiles

Pictures and profiles save on your device. They do not sync between devices. Deleting the app or deleting local data removes this saved content, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your drawings.

Playing together

A grown-up can help with animal clues and choose activities in the Parent Zone. Coloring includes freehand drawing and tap-to-fill, along with a blank page for new ideas.
